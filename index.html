From discovery to decision

How to use an OrientDig spreadsheet

A product directory helps you organize ideas. The important decisions come from checking the actual seller listing, your chosen agent’s order screen and the full delivery cost.

Build a shortlist you can verify

  1. Find an item. Search a descriptive term, filter the selection and note the catalog item number.
  2. Inspect the source. Open HipobuyFind, locate the item and compare its photos, seller link and available options.
  3. Confirm agent support. If you plan to use OrientDig, check the matching listing and current purchasing restrictions there before paying.
  4. Check measurements. Compare the seller’s size chart with an item you already own. A familiar size label alone is not enough.
  5. Review your order. Confirm the chosen variant, inspect any available order-specific QC photos and review the shipping quote.

Before you pay

What photos and prices can tell you

Product photos show the seller’s presentation. QC photos, when available, show a particular inspected item. Neither establishes the condition of a future order. Look for clear views of seams, soles, labels, dimensions and the exact color you selected.

For shoes, compare the size chart and any available insole measurements. For clothing, check chest width and garment length. For bags, compare dimensions and closures. If an important detail is missing, request clarification through the purchasing service before proceeding.

Check return conditions before ordering and again before international dispatch. Seller availability, return windows and transport restrictions can affect whether an item is suitable for your order.

Clear answers

OrientDig spreadsheet FAQ

What is an OrientDig spreadsheet?

An OrientDig spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize marketplace finds before buying through a shopping agent. This independent page presents a static selection from HipobuyFind, with category filters and reference prices.

Is this the official OrientDig website?

No. This is an independent product discovery guide. Its shopping buttons open HipobuyFind, a separate catalog website.

Are the displayed prices the delivered cost?

No. Listed item prices and approximate USD references exclude any additional domestic delivery, agent fees, international shipping and applicable taxes. Confirm the complete total before paying.

Does every product include QC photos?

QC availability has not been verified for this selection. The images displayed here are catalog product photos. If inspection images are available elsewhere, check which item and order they belong to.

Can I buy directly on this page?

No checkout or payment is provided here. Open HipobuyFind to locate a source listing, then review the available purchasing services and their current terms.

Does the directory update automatically?

No. This page contains six listings from the dated source snapshot. Search and sorting work locally on that selection. Open the source catalog for current listings, options and prices.

Do I need to download a spreadsheet?

No download is required to browse these cards. Search by product type or item number, choose a category and sort by the displayed CNY item price.
